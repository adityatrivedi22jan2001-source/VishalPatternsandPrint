01 • Who We Are

Three generations. One textile journey.

In 1978, Harish B. Trivedi began with hand block printing at about 22 years of age, with the support of his wife Damyati Trivedi. The business grew from that small beginning into a wider textile manufacturing operation as the next generation joined.

Today, Vikram H. Trivedi, Vishal H. Trivedi and Piyush S. Trivedi carry the work forward across design, development, production, departmental planning and factory management.
