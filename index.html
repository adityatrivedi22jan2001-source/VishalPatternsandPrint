Who we are

Started at a wooden table, in 1978.

Harish B. Trivedi was around 22 when he began hand block printing, with his wife Damyati beside him. Decades later, his sons — Vikram, Vishal and Piyush — turned that beginning into a full manufacturing operation: design, printing, embroidery, value addition, quality control and finishing, all under one roof in Vasai.

What we do

Printing is only where it starts.

One saree can move through printing, embroidery, Aari work and finishing before it's ready for a wholesaler.

01

Screen & Pigment Printing

Production-scale fabric printing for sarees and dress material, run on long tables by hand.

02

Digital Sublimation

Fine, detailed printing for contemporary designs alongside our traditional-style work.

03

Computer Embroidery

Machine embroidery for borders, motifs and value-added surface work.

04

Aari & Chain Stitch

Hand chain-stitch and blanket-stitch work — chawal taka and faat taka — on sarees.

05

Value Addition

Print with embroidery, digital print with Aari, gold print, border attachment — combinations built around the order.

06

Stitching & Finishing

Jhalar and border attachment, final checking, packing and dispatch preparation.
