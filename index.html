Who We Are

A family-built textile manufacturing journey.

In 1978, Harish B. Trivedi began with block printing. What started as a small, hands-on operation grew through the work of his family and team into a multi-process textile manufacturing business serving wholesalers and textile traders.

Today, our work spans screen printing, computer embroidery, digital sublimation printing, hand block printing, Aari and stitch work, borders, jhalars and other value additions. We specialise particularly in sarees and also work with kurties, dress materials and printed fabric lumps.

1978

The beginning — block printing

Harish B. Trivedi started the business at a young age with block printing, working closely with his wife Damyati Trivedi.

Early Years

Craft, colour and repetition

Printing was hands-on. Blocks, colour preparation, tables and careful placement were central to producing saree designs.

Next Generation

Vikram & Vishal join the work

The next generation expanded the operation, developing design, client coordination, production and day-to-day factory management.

Today

Multiple processes, one manufacturing flow

Screen printing, computer embroidery, digital sublimation, traditional printing and value-addition work now operate together with process-level quality checks.

What We Do

Printing, embroidery
& value addition.

Our strength is not one machine or one printing method. It is the ability to take a textile requirement through multiple stages and coordinate the finished result.

01

Screen Printing

Pigment printing on fabric with experience in traditional Indian-style saree and textile designs.

02

Digital Sublimation

Digital sublimation printing for polyester fabrics, designs and printed fabric requirements.

03

Computer Embroidery

Computer embroidery and embroidery-led value addition for sarees and other textile products.

04

Traditional & Aari Work

Hand block printing, Aari / chain stitch, blanket stitch and combinations of print with embroidery work.

Quality Control

Quality is checked throughout the flow.

We do not treat checking as a final-stage formality. Our process includes quality control after each major stage so defects can be identified before they move further into production.

01

Process Check

Inspect the textile and print work after the relevant production process.

02

Work / Value Addition Check

Check embroidery, Aari, borders, jhalars and other additional work.

03

Final Goods Check

Final inspection before packing and wholesale dispatch.
