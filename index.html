01 • Who we are

A family-built textile journey.

Vishal Patterns & Print Pvt. Ltd. began in 1979 with Harish B. Trivedi and a hands-on approach to textile printing. What started as a small operation grew through years of learning the fabric, the print and the demands of the customer.

Today, the company carries that foundation into a broader manufacturing environment, combining traditional printing knowledge with design development, production and problem-solving across the factory.

1979

The first chapter — a small beginning built around textile printing and craftsmanship.

2nd Gen.
