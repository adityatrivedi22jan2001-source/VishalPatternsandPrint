How decisions get made

Design, floor and flow — split three ways.

Design and client-facing decisions sit with Vikram. Machinery, electrical work and factory operations sit with Vishal. Piyush keeps departments in sequence — printing feeding embroidery feeding finishing, without a unit sitting idle or a unit falling behind. Between the three, the factory runs as one operation rather than separate departments.
