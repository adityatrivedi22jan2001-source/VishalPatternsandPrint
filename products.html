Products & applications

Sarees

Our strongest specialisation.

Printed sarees, value-added sarees, borders, jhalars, embroidery, Aari work, faat taka, gold print, and combinations of print with surface work — built to the design brief a trader brings us.

Kurti dress material

Traditional design language for modern trade.

We work with Indian traditional-style design directions — Batik, Kalamkari, block, Jamdani, Kashmiri and Madhubani influences among them — and hold a wide range of design possibilities across our printing and value-addition capabilities. We can also supply contrast or work blouse pieces to pair with plain or lightly printed sarees.
