Our journey

1978 to today.

Told through the photographs the family kept — from a single block table to a factory floor of two hundred hands.

1978

The first block

Harish B. Trivedi begins the textile journey with hand block printing, at around 22 years of age, with his wife Damyati Trivedi beside him.

Early era

Learning the craft

Printing, colour mixing, fabric knowledge and client relationships grow through hands-on work at the first small workshop near Mahalaxmi. The faded photographs on this site belong to this chapter.

Next gen.

Vikram & Vishal join

The business expands beyond block printing into screen printing, embroidery, digital printing and broader manufacturing capability as the sons take on design, client handling and production.

Growth

Piyush joins the production flow

Departmental programming and production coordination become part of the growing factory operation, as Piyush S. Trivedi takes on the flow of work between units.

Today

A wholesale manufacturing house

Design through finished packing, with printing, embroidery, value addition and QC running across the workflow — still family-run, now at a scale of two hundred hands at peak.

In photographs

Two eras, side by side.

Older, faded photographs belong to the Harish B. Trivedi founding era. Colour photographs belong to the Vikram, Vishal and Piyush era — the expansion of capability and the growth of the factory.
