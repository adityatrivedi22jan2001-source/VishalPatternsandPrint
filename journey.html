Our Journey

1978 → Today.

Read the company history through the photographs and the generations that built it.

1978

The first block

Harish B. Trivedi begins the textile journey with hand block printing at around 22 years of age, supported by his wife Damyati Trivedi.

Early era

Learning the craft

Printing, fabric knowledge, design and relationships grow through hands-on work. The faded photographs belong to this chapter.

Next generation

Vikram & Vishal join

The business expands beyond block printing into screen printing, embroidery, digital printing and broader manufacturing capabilities.

Growth

Piyush joins the production flow

Departmental programming and production coordination become part of the growing factory operation.

Today

A wholesale manufacturing house

Design through finished packing, with printing, embroidery, value addition and QC across the workflow.

Historical photo grouping: older/faded photographs = Harish B. Trivedi founding era; later colour photographs = Vikram, Vishal and Piyush era.
