Who we are

Built from a small beginning in 1978.

A textile story that started with one man and a block of carved wood, and grew into a family-run manufacturing house.

The founder

Harish B. Trivedi began with block printing.

In 1978, at around 22 years old, Harish B. Trivedi started the textile journey with hand block printing. His wife, Damyati Trivedi, was beside him in those early years — learning the fabric, the colours and the trade together.

We keep the old photographs on this site on purpose. They're evidence of where the business actually came from, before modern machinery, before the current scale — a reminder of what the company was built on.

Left: Harish B. Trivedi at the block table. Right: teaching colour mixing; hand block printing in process.

The family, in the first factory — the same hanging fabric this company still makes today.

What we are today

A wholesale textile manufacturing partner.

We work with wholesalers and textile traders, with a strong specialisation in sarees and kurti dress material. Our work spans design, fabric, printing, embroidery, value addition, quality control, finishing and packing.

We don't position ourselves as a retail brand. We manufacture for the trade — nothing sold direct, nothing retail-facing.
