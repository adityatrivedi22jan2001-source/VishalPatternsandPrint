Who We Are

Built from a small beginning in 1978.

A textile story started by Harish B. Trivedi and carried forward across generations.

The Founder

Harish B. Trivedi began with block printing.

In 1978, at around 22 years old, Harish B. Trivedi started the textile journey with hand block printing. His wife, Damyati Trivedi, was beside him in those early years.

The old photographs are deliberately shown as part of the story. They are evidence of where the business came from — before modern printing equipment, before today's scale and before the company became what it is now.

What we are today

A wholesale textile manufacturing partner.

We work with wholesalers and textile traders, with a strong specialization in sarees and kurti dress material. Our work spans design, fabric, printing, embroidery, value addition, QC, finishing and packing.

We do not position ourselves as a retail brand. We manufacture for the trade.
