Capabilities

One order can need many processes.

Printing, embroidery, stitching, design and value addition, combined under a single manufacturing workflow.

01

Screen & Pigment Printing

Fabric screen printing and pigment printing for sarees, dress material and other textile applications.

02

Digital Sublimation

Detailed digital sublimation printing for contemporary and traditional-style designs.

03

Computer Embroidery

Embroidery machinery for borders, motifs and value-added textile work.

04

Aari & Chain Stitch

Traditional chain stitch and Aari work for sarees and other textile surfaces.

05

Blanket & Faat Taka

Chawal taka and faat taka style work, done as value-added decoration.

06

Gold Printing

Gold print and other decorative combinations, built around the design brief.

Hand block printing, still done by hand on long tables — the same technique the company started with in 1978.

Value addition

Print plus work. Digital plus embroidery.

Most orders combine more than one process. Print with embroidery. Print with an embroidered border attached. Digital print with Aari work. Digital print with faat taka. Gold print layered over a base pattern. We build the combination around what the client's order actually needs, rather than offering one fixed finish.
